Hold the close until the count matches.

The close depends on a spoken count.

Before the wound is closed, the circulating nurse reconciles sponges that went in with sponges that came out. A sponge still in the field and a tray that was misread are both ways that close happens too early. One nurse may be covering more than one room. OR Sentinel does not replace that count. It refuses to treat a mismatch as a clean close.
