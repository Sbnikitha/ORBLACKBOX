ZRT serves both models on the Nano. The 27B reads the room. The vision model reads the tray. Fixed rules decide the close. The cloud only sees an anonymous protocol question, and only when the local model is unsure.

One floor. Eight rooms. Close stays locked until the count is clean.

The circulating nurse owns the sponge count. OR Sentinel listens to the room and looks at the tray. Speech updates how many sponges went in and came out, and which WHO checklist steps were said. The camera counts what is actually on the tray. Fixed rules, not the model, choose SAFE, CAUTION, or BLOCK. The Critic only explains that decision in one sentence.

Audio

Counter reads added and removed. Checklist tags time-out, patient, site, allergies, count confirmed, and close.

Camera

When a sponge comes out, TrayCount reads a tray frame. The hidden room shows one fewer sponge than the nurse said.

Rules

BLOCK only if someone asks to close and the spoken count is short or the camera disagrees. A missing time-out is CAUTION.

What the eight rooms do

  • Rooms 1 and 4 play a complete appendectomy. Expect SAFE.
  • Rooms 2, 5, and 8 say ten sponges in and nine out. Expect BLOCK.
  • Rooms 3 and 6 never do the time-out. Expect CAUTION.
  • Room 7, the default hidden tray, sounds balanced and the camera sees nine. Expect BLOCK.

Why the block is not an AI mood

If the model misreads a line, the rule still needs a close request plus a real mismatch. The nurse’s count stays official. Killing the camera drops the room to audio only. Cutting the cloud keeps every urgent and every identifiable question on the box. Forging the ledger lights the seal, because the hash no longer matches.

Edge versus cloud

Primary inference stays here. The cloud is a protocol desk for anonymous, non-urgent questions the local model is unsure about. Names, dates of birth, ages, and record numbers are replaced before the send, then checked again. If anything remains, nothing is sent.

Synthetic acted cases and generated trays. Not a medical device. Not for clinical use. The circulating nurse’s count stays the record of truth.
