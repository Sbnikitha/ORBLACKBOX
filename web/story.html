THE STORY

After Air India, the answer was the recorder.

Investigators did not need another guess. They needed the black box: a sealed record of what actually happened. An operating room needs that kind of record before anyone is harmed. This story has no casualty. The win is a close that waits, and a log that cannot be quietly rewritten.

Each model has its own port on 100.81.221.41, so one job cannot block the others. The bars show a round trip versus staying in the room. They are not a measured time. If the Nano does not answer, a script on this machine still reads the spoken line. The close rule does not wait for ZRT.

WHY NOT THESE

Other paths send the room away, or let the model lock it.

Cloud modelAdds a round trip. To answer, it would need the audio, the photo, or the chart. That is the wait and the compliance failure.
One model for allA language model can explain a mismatch. It must not be the lock. Only the rule can say BLOCK.
Nano, per portThe hackathon box. Each model is served next to the room. A laptop with no ZRT is only a booth.

The cloud is a protocol desk for a stripped, non-urgent question. It is not the sponge count, and it is not the close.
